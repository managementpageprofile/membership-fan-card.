## Before publishing
1. Replace placeholder event information with your real, authorized events.
2. Add only celebrity/artist names, logos and photos you are authorized to use.
3. Review the membership prices and exact benefits.
4. Replace the general refund language with your actual purchase/refund rules.
5. Review the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy for the jurisdiction(s) where you operate.
6. Consider using a dedicated transactional/form provider rather than relying on a basic email-forwarding service for sensitive information.
